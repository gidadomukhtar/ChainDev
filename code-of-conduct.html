Our community standards

Code of Conduct.

ChainDev is built on learning, collaboration and shared opportunities. This Code of Conduct explains the behaviour we expect from everyone and how we respond when those expectations are not met.

Our commitment

We want ChainDev to be a community where people can learn, build, ask questions, share ideas and collaborate without fear of harassment, discrimination or humiliation. Everyone has a responsibility to help maintain that environment.

1. Who this applies to

This Code of Conduct applies to all ChainDev members, founders, organisers, mentors, speakers, volunteers, contributors and guests.

It applies across ChainDev activities and spaces, including:

  • Physical meetups, workshops, hackathons and other events.
  • Online meetings, discussions and learning sessions.
  • Official community communication channels.
  • Community projects, collaborations and activities.
  • Situations where someone represents ChainDev publicly.

2. Expected behaviour

Everyone in ChainDev is expected to:

  • Treat others with respect, patience and consideration.
  • Welcome people regardless of their background, discipline, experience level or location.
  • Support beginners and avoid making anyone feel inadequate for asking questions or not knowing something.
  • Communicate constructively, even when opinions differ.
  • Respect personal boundaries, privacy and individual differences.
  • Give appropriate credit when using or building on another person's work.
  • Collaborate honestly and take responsibility for mistakes.
  • Help create an environment where people feel comfortable contributing and learning.

3. Unacceptable behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable in ChainDev spaces:

  • Harassment, bullying, intimidation, threats or personal attacks.
  • Discrimination or hateful comments based on someone's identity, background or personal characteristics.
  • Humiliating, insulting or deliberately discouraging beginners.
  • Unwanted sexual attention, advances or inappropriate contact.
  • Sharing private information, photographs or conversations without permission.
  • Impersonation, scams, malicious links or deliberate attempts to deceive community members.
  • Abusing a leadership, mentorship or organisational position.
  • Deliberately disrupting community activities or preventing others from participating.
  • Retaliating against anyone who raises a concern or participates in a report.

4. Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to ChainDev leadership. You do not need to confront the person involved before submitting a report.

Official reporting contact

Send your report to the ChainDev community leadership:

chaindevcomm@gmail.com

What to include in a report

Please provide as much relevant information as possible:

  • What happened and when it happened.
  • Where it occurred, such as an event or online platform.
  • The people involved, if known.
  • Any supporting evidence, such as messages or screenshots, where available.

How reports are handled

Reports will be taken seriously and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Access will be limited to authorised ChainDev representatives responsible for addressing the concern.

Where possible, someone who is not directly involved in the reported incident will help handle the matter. People who submit reports in good faith will not be punished or intimidated for speaking up.

If there is an immediate threat to someone's safety, contact the appropriate local emergency or security services.

5. Consequences of unacceptable behaviour

Depending on the seriousness and circumstances of an incident, ChainDev may take one or more of the following actions:

  • A private warning and request to stop the behaviour.
  • Removal from a discussion or community activity.
  • Removal from a particular event or session.
  • Temporary suspension from community spaces.
  • Removal from a leadership, mentoring or volunteer role.
  • Permanent exclusion from ChainDev.

Serious incidents may result in immediate action. Where appropriate, matters may also be referred to relevant authorities.

Actions will be considered based on the available information, the seriousness of the conduct and the need to protect community members.

6. Accountability and commitment

ChainDev leadership is also bound by this Code of Conduct. No founder, organiser, mentor or volunteer is exempt from these expectations.

We recognise that building an inclusive community requires continuous effort. We welcome constructive feedback and remain committed to improving how people experience and participate in ChainDev.

By participating in ChainDev activities and spaces, you agree to respect these community standards.
