Why ChainDev exists

Talent is everywhere. Access to opportunity should be, too.

ChainDev began with a simple belief: where someone lives or where they are starting from should not determine how far they can go in technology. We are building a community where curiosity can grow into skills, collaboration and meaningful contributions.

01 / LEARN

Learn with others

Beginner-friendly learning sessions, shared resources and peer support that make technology more approachable.

02 / BUILD

Build practical skills

Collaborative projects, coding activities and hands-on experiences that turn knowledge into practice.

03 / CONNECT

Find your people

Meet learners, developers, mentors and communities who can help you grow and contribute.

04 / CONTRIBUTE

Create impact

Use technology and shared knowledge to work on challenges that matter to people and communities.

What we do

A place to move from learning to doing.

Learning and community sessions

We create opportunities to explore software development, open source, cloud, AI, cybersecurity and other areas of technology through accessible learning activities.

Projects and collaboration

We encourage members to learn together, exchange ideas, contribute to projects and develop the confidence to participate in the wider developer ecosystem.

Events and hackathons

We support practical events where people can meet, experiment, collaborate and learn by building.

Opportunities and pathways

We share useful resources and pathways into open source, mentorship, developer programs, learning opportunities and the global tech community.
